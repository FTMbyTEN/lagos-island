Payout terms

Lagos Island · last updated 21 October 2026

  • Real money comes only from funded jobs. Game naira (from shifts, jobs, the National Grid and purchases) stay in the game and can never be withdrawn.
  • Withdrawals: at least ₦5,000 and at most ₦25,000 each, up to ₦50,000 per player in 24 hours.
  • Every withdrawal is checked for 12 hours before it is sent to your bank.
  • The bank transfer fee is paid by you and taken from the amount; the game shows exactly what you will receive before you confirm.
  • You must be 18 or older, with a Nigerian bank account in your own name.

1. Who can be paid

To withdraw real earnings you must be at least 18 years old, and the bank account you add must be a Nigerian account held in your own legal name. When you add it, we ask your bank (through our payment provider, Flutterwave) for the name on the account; it must match the name you give us. You also confirm your email address with a code. One payout account per player.

2. What earns real money

Only funded jobs earn real money: paid gigs in the game with a cash reward set aside in advance by their sponsor. Each one shows its cash reward before you take it. A cash reward is first pending while the work is checked, then becomes available to withdraw. Everything else in Lagos Island pays in game naira, which has no cash value.

3. Withdrawing

  • The least you can withdraw at once is ₦5,000; the most is ₦25,000; and no more than ₦50,000 in any 24 hours.
  • Withdrawals are paid only to your verified payout account.
  • Each withdrawal waits 12 hours for review before it is sent. During that time we may stop it if something looks wrong; if we do, the full amount goes back to your available earnings.
  • Once sent, most transfers arrive within minutes, but your bank may take longer.

4. Fees

Bank transfers carry a fee charged by the payment provider. You pay this fee: it is taken from the amount you withdraw, and the game shows the fee and exactly what you will receive before you confirm.

5. Failed and returned transfers

If a transfer fails, or your bank sends it back, the amount returns to your available earnings and you can withdraw it again. A fee already charged by the provider for a returned transfer may not be refunded.

6. Fair play

Cheating, using more than one account to earn the same reward, faking work, or adding a bank account that isn't yours may lead to earnings being withheld, withdrawals being stopped, and the account being closed. We keep records of earnings and withdrawals to check this.

7. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for any tax that applies to money you earn. Keep your account details private: never share your password or the codes we send you.

8. Changes and pauses

We may pause withdrawals for a short time (for example to fix a problem); your earnings stay safe while they are paused. We may change these terms; when we do, we will tell you in the game, and you may need to accept the new terms before your next withdrawal.

9. Contact

Questions about a payout? Ask WYRD in the game, quoting your withdrawal reference (it starts with FJW-).
